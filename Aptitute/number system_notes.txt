Numbers are the fundamental units of mathematics. In this article, we will discuss numbers, their types, important facts, divisibility rules, and other important points about numbers for aptitude preparation.

Types of Numbers
Integers: All numbers whose fractional part is 0 (zero), like -3, -2, 1, 0, 10, and 100, are integers.
Natural Numbers: Counting numbers like 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 ... Basically, all integers greater than 0 are natural numbers.
Whole Numbers: All natural numbers and 0 (zero) are whole numbers.
Prime Numbers: All numbers having only two distinct factors, the number itself and 1, are called prime numbers. Some prime numbers are 2, 3, 53, 67, and 191.
Composite Number: All numbers greater than 1 that are NOT prime are composite numbers. Some composite numbers are 4, 60, 91, and 100.

Other Useful Rules

If we repeat a three-digit number twice, to form a six-digit number. The result will be divisible by 7, 11, and 13, and dividing by all three will give your original three-digit number.
A number of form 2N has exactly N+1 divisors.
For example: 4 has 3 divisors, 1, 2, and 4.
